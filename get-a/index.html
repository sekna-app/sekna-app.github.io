About this app

SEKNA turns the media plan into a live dashboard. Spend, conversions, conversion value and ROAS for every day, checked against the plan, with the campaign types, projects and keywords behind them.

Compare any month, see the same Hijri day in past years, and share any view to WhatsApp as a clean image with a ready-made caption.

The app updates itself: new features appear the next time you open it, with nothing to re-install.
