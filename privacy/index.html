Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

HEMERA is a private dashboard for invited team members. Here is what it uses, in short.

1What we use

  • Your Google sign-in: name, email and photo, to know who you are.
  • Your team's sheets: the media plan and results, to show the dashboard.
  • Notifications: a device token, only if you allow them.
  • App usage: opens and time spent, for the owner only.

2What we never do

  • Sell or share your data.
  • Show ads or track you on other apps and sites.

3Where it runs

On Google: Sign-In, Sheets and Firebase. Everything is sent over HTTPS, and every request is checked against your role.

4Your choices

  • Turn notifications off or sign out at any time.
  • Ask us to see, fix or delete your data, or remove your access.
