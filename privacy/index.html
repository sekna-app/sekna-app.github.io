Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

HEMERA is a private media plan dashboard made by Goldline Studio for invited team members only. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and how it is kept safe. We keep it short and plain.

1Who can use HEMERA

HEMERA is not open to the public. Only people the team owner approves can sign in. Each person sees only the pages their role allows.

2What we use

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile picture from your Google sign-in, to know who you are and what you may see.
  • Team data: the media plan, campaign results and reports that your organisation keeps in its own Google Sheets. HEMERA reads them to show the dashboard and, for permitted roles, writes plan changes back.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a notification token for your device, so we can send you the daily numbers, season reminders and task alerts.
  • App usage: simple counts such as when the app was opened, how long it was used and which pages were shared, so the owner can see the app is useful. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Settings on your device: choices such as currency, theme and the last account you opened are saved on your own device.

3What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We do not share your information with anyone outside your organisation, except the services that run the app (below).

4Services that run the app

HEMERA runs on Google services: Google Sign-In, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, Google Firebase (hosting, server, storage and notifications) and Gmail for team emails. They process data on our behalf under Google's own privacy terms.

5How long we keep it

Your account stays while you are a member of the team. Usage counts are kept for as long as they help the team, and are removed when no longer needed. Team data stays in your organisation's own sheets.

6Keeping it safe

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request is checked against your sign-in and your role, and owner-only pages stay owner-only.

7Your choices

  • You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings.
  • You can sign out at any time, and clear the app's data from your browser or device.
  • You can ask the team owner to see, correct or delete the information held about you, or to remove your access.

8Children

HEMERA is a work tool for adults and is not meant for children.

9Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

10Contact

Questions about your information, or want something removed? Email us at seknaapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.
