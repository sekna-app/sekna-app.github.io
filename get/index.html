What is SEKNA?

SEKNA turns the media plan into a live dashboard. Every day it shows spend, conversions, conversion value and ROAS against the daily target, split by campaign type, with the projects and keywords behind them.

Follow the month day by day, compare any two months, see how the same Hijri day did in past years, and share any view to WhatsApp as a clean image. The same live numbers on Android, iPhone and your laptop.
